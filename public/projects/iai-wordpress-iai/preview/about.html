About The Golden Crumb

A fictional cookie shop website built for an Informática Aplicada a Internet (IAI) school project. Every page uses WordPress blocks, custom theme styling, and sample content in English.

Our project story

The Golden Crumb imagines a neighborhood bakery where every cookie is baked in small batches. This site demonstrates clear navigation, visual hierarchy, and accessible content.

  • Home page with menu and customer reviews
  • About page explaining the school project
  • Contact page with a working inquiry form
  • Images and embedded video for richer media
